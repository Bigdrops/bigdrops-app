01Problem reported

Major scheduled top and bottom end overhaul at 30,000 running hours, high blow-by, exhaust port carbon fouling, and intermittent cylinder misfires under peak load.

02Service rendered

Conducted complete major engine overhaul: stripped down cylinder heads, replaced all cylinder liners, pistons, rings, wrist pins, con-rod bearings, and main crankshaft bearings. Rebuilt and leak-tested cylinder heads with new valves, seats, guides, and rotocaps. Replaced spark plugs, ignition extensions, ignition leads, and calibrated detonator sensors. Overhauled both turbochargers, replaced charge air cooler cores, inspected wastegate actuator linkages, aligned throttle body actuator, recalibrated fuel gas control valve and mixer, renewed all external water/oil plumbing elastomeric seals, replaced crankcase breather assemblies, flushed lube oil galleries, refilled with fresh low-ash gas engine oil, conducted engine bar-over, pre-lube check, cold commissioning, step load testing from 25% to 100% (2000 kW), and dynamic emission tuning to maintain NOx within operational limits.

03Defects found

Severe carbon deposition across all 18 cylinder exhaust valves; excessive side clearance on top compression ring grooves on Cylinders #4, #9, and #14; moderate cavitation erosion on cylinder liner lower O-ring register lands (Cylinders #3, #7); heat stress cracking on two ignition lead boots; minor axial play (0.18 mm) on secondary turbocharger shaft exceeding service limit; worn throttle spindle shaft seals causing air leakage.

04Engineer remarks

Major mechanical overhaul completed to manufacturer specifications. Engine barring torque smooth and within normal tolerance. Full synthetic low-ash SAE 40 lubricant loaded. Dynamic load banking achieved 500 kW, 1000 kW, 1500 kW, and sustained 2000 kW at 0.8 PF for 4 hours continuously. All bearing temperatures, exhaust port temperatures, and oil pressure profiles stabilized within OEM tolerances. Unit re-commissioned, auto-synchronization with plant 11kV bus verified, and returned to active baseload service.
