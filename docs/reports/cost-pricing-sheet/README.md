# Cost & Pricing Sheet Reports — Orientation

The document this folder covers was previously called BOQ in BIGDROPS.
Product review determined that "Bill of Quantities" does not describe its
actual role: internal cost construction, CP/SP capture, profit and margin
analysis, commercial preparation, and conversion into a customer-facing
Quotation. The canonical name is now Cost & Pricing Sheet.

Historical reports in this folder may still use "BOQ" in filenames and
content. Interpret those references as the predecessor terminology for the
Cost & Pricing Sheet, unless a report explicitly discusses conventional BOQ
semantics. The reports were intentionally not rewritten. They are audit and
design evidence from the time they were produced.

Current and future reports use Cost & Pricing Sheet terminology. Production
identifiers (`boqs`, `boq_rows`, routes, types, audit entities) remain legacy
`boq` names until a separately authorized implementation task decides otherwise.
