# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

## 1. Introduction

BigDrops is a B2B business management suite that helps Nigerian SMEs manage invoices, quotations, waybills, customers, suppliers, and related business operations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our platform.

## 2. Data We Collect

**Account Information**
- Email address, name, company name, phone number
- Login credentials (hashed and stored securely)

**Business Data**
- Invoices, quotations, waybills, purchase orders, and other business documents you create
- Customer and supplier records
- Product/service catalogs
- Financial transaction data (amounts, VAT, totals, payment status)

**Usage Data**
- Feature usage patterns
- Login times and session duration
- Device and browser information
- Error logs and performance data

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- To operate and maintain the platform
- To generate, store, and retrieve business documents
- To sync data across your devices (web and mobile)
- To provide customer support
- To improve product features and user experience
- To send important service notifications (security alerts, account updates)

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Access controlled via Row Level Security (RLS) — each organization can only access its own data
- Authentication managed via secure session tokens
- Regular backups to prevent data loss
- We do not store payment card details; payments are processed by third-party providers

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers who may process your data on our behalf:

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Supabase | Database hosting and authentication |
| Vercel | Web application hosting |
| Capacitor | Mobile app framework |

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 6. Data Retention

- Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active
- You may delete specific documents or your entire account at any time
- Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days
- Backup copies are purged within 90 days

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access and review all data we store about your organization
- Edit or correct inaccurate information
- Export your data in standard formats
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Request information about how your data is processed

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

## 8. Compliance

This platform is designed to comply with the **Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)**. We process personal data lawfully, fairly, and with transparency. Data is collected for specified, legitimate purposes and is not retained longer than necessary.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for business use and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app notification. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
