What changed

  • KPI grid — stays static, no auto-scroll/cube confirmed
  • Payment nag — replaced the "3rd slide" idea with a smart banner under the KPI grid. Only shows when there's an actual mismatch (balance moved, no payment logged). Dismissible.
  • "Do you know" tips — moved off the dashboard entirely, onto the loading screen. Quotation→invoice conversion is the first example tip.
  • Sidebar — tap ☰: drawer slides in from the left with a scrim behind it, 6 nav placeholders + footer
  • Notifications — tap 🔔: dropdown panel anchored under the bell, unread items marked with a red dot
  • AI assistant — tap the avatar: bottom sheet slides up from the nav bar, chat bubbles + input row, labeled "on-device" per your local LLM setup

Opening the sidebar, notifications, or AI panel auto-closes the other two and the FAB popup — only one overlay at a time. Click the scrim or press Escape to close.
