Problem Reported
Operations reported repeated generator shutdowns during transfer under peak process loading. Alarm history indicated unstable charging voltage, intermittent low oil pressure warnings and delayed governor response after automatic start requests. Unit occasionally recovered after manual restart but experienced erratic engine speed and abnormal exhaust smoke. Maintenance support was requested to restore dependable standby power while avoiding disruption to continuous production activities and scheduled utility switching.
Defects Found
Inspection confirmed contaminated fuel filters, weakened alternator drive belt tension, oxidized battery terminals and partially obstructed radiator fins restricting airflow. Governor linkage required calibration while engine oil showed reduced viscosity beyond maintenance tolerance. Minor coolant seepage was detected around an upper hose clamp although no structural damage to the cooling assembly or engine block was observed.
Service Rendered
Isolation procedures were completed before comprehensive inspection, diagnostic scanning and operational verification. Fuel and lubrication systems were serviced, worn consumables replaced, electrical connections cleaned and tightened, charging output confirmed and governor calibration adjusted to specification. Cooling passages were cleaned, belt tension corrected and coolant leak rectified with new clamp hardware. Functional testing included manual and automatic starts, staged load acceptance, voltage stability checks, frequency validation and sixty-minute monitored operation. Final commissioning confirmed reliable performance without recurring alarms or abnormal operating behaviour.

Engineer Remarks
Generator performance has been restored to acceptable operating limits. Recommend quarterly oil analysis, monthly battery load testing, radiator cleaning after dusty operating periods and replacement of fuel filters at scheduled intervals irrespective of apparent condition. Review transfer switch event logs during preventive maintenance to identify early electrical abnormalities before operational impact.
Customer Feedback
Service team responded promptly, communicated findings clearly and completed repairs within the agreed maintenance window. Equipment returned to production without further interruptions during monitored operation. Documentation and housekeeping met site expectations and recommendations will be incorporated into the preventive maintenance programme.
