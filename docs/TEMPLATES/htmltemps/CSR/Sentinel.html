Problem Reported
Intermittent shutdown during peak load periods over the preceding two weeks. The generator trips on emergency stop without warning, particularly when load exceeds 380 kVA. Client reports voltage fluctuations prior to shutdown and occasional black smoke from exhaust during restart attempts.
Defects Found
  1. Faulty Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR): The AVR module (Caterpillar OEM # 123-4567) exhibits erratic output — measured 185V at excitation coil under load, well below the 220V specification, causing voltage sag and eventual under-voltage trip.
  2. Worn Fuel Injectors (Cylinders #3 & #5): Injectors showed excessive back-leakage of 18% and 22% respectively (spec < 8%), causing incomplete combustion, black smoke, and load-dependent misfiring.
  3. Clogged Primary Fuel Filter / Water Separator: Element heavily contaminated with sludge and water ingress — fuel sample tested positive for microbial growth (diesel bug), restricting flow and starving the injection pump at high demand.
  4. Loose Exhaust Manifold Bolts (Bank A): Three manifold bolts found torqued to approximately 25 Nm instead of the required 65 Nm, resulting in an exhaust gas leak, turbocharger whistle, and elevated under-hood temperature.
Service Rendered
1. Diagnostics & Load Testing: Conducted full load-bank test up to 450 kVA. Monitored voltage, frequency, and temperature profiles. Identified AVR instability above 60% load — confirmed faulty regulation.   2. AVR Replacement: Removed and replaced the defective AVR module with a new Caterpillar OEM unit (P/N 123-4567). Recalibrated voltage setpoint to 415V ± 1% at no-load and full-load conditions.   3. Injector Service: Replaced fuel injectors on cylinders #3 and #5 with OEM C15 units. Installed new injector seal kits and torqued hold-down bolts to 55 Nm per specification.   4. Fuel System Overhaul: Replaced both primary and secondary fuel filters. Drained and cleaned the water separator bowl. Dosed the fuel tank with a diesel biocide treatment. Ran fuel polishing cycle for 30 minutes.   5. Exhaust Manifold Rectification: Re-torqued all Bank A exhaust manifold bolts to 65 Nm. Replaced two manifold gaskets showing signs of blow-by.   6. Final Verification: Full-load test at 450 kVA for 60 minutes. All parameters stable: Voltage 415 V ± 1.2%, Frequency 50 Hz ± 0.3 Hz, Temperature 87 °C, Oil Pressure 46 PSI. Unit handed over in satisfactory condition.

Engineer Remarks
The generator has been returned to service in satisfactory condition. The root cause of the intermittent shutdown was traced to the failing AVR combined with clogged fuel delivery components. It is recommended that the fuel storage tanks be inspected and cleaned within the next 30 days to prevent recurrence of microbial contamination. Additionally, a predictive maintenance schedule should be adopted — including quarterly fuel analysis and annual injector back-leak testing — to maximise service life. The unit should be re-tested under full load after 50 operational hours to confirm injector seating and AVR stability.
