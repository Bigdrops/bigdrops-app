Problem Reported
Gradual loss of discharge pressure over a three-month period, from nominal 125 PSI down to 78 PSI under load, accompanied by increased oil consumption and elevated operating temperature exceeding 85 °C during extended runtime.
Defects Found
  • Worn Intake Valve Seals — Leakage detected during compression cycle causing pressure loss and oil carryover into discharge line
  • Clogged Oil Separator Element — Restricted flow with 0.8 bar pressure drop across element, well above 0.3 bar OEM threshold
  • Failed Minimum Pressure Valve — Unable to maintain receiver pressure below 80 PSI, causing rapid system cycling and wear
  • Degraded Coolant Condition — Reduced thermal conductivity with 15% glycol imbalance and visible particulate contamination
Service Rendered
  • Conducted full system diagnostics: pressure/flow tests, thermal imaging scan, and vibration analysis
  • Replaced worn intake valve seals and fitted new minimum pressure valve assembly
  • Changed oil filter, air filter, and separator element per OEM maintenance schedule
  • Flushed coolant system and recharged with fresh coolant/antifreeze mixture at correct concentration
  • Replaced thermostatic valve and all gaskets/O-rings during reassembly
  • Calibrated pressure switches, safety valves, and controller parameters to OEM specifications
  • Performed full load test, leak-down test, and vibration analysis — all results within OEM tolerances
  • System recommissioned and restored to full operational status at 125 PSI discharge pressure
