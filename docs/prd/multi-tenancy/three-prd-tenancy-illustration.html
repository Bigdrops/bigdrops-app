Three-PRD Tenancy Ledger

Platform Office governs workspace lifecycle only. The ERP owns entity creation, invite acceptance, tenant routing and create-or-join onboarding. Multi-workspace membership is allowed, with exactly one workspace active per session. Roles are editable ability bundles: Workspace Admin is the workspace-wide authority ceiling; company-scoped roles (including Company Admin) sit below it, bounded per company; only expanded entity_permissions rows authorize anything — a role label has no authority of its own. Business data lives inside the resolved entity schema, protected by explicit permission grants — click through the states below.
